Key Findings

01
Motivation collapses after 10 days Users report strong initial enthusiasm but lose motivation when they miss a single session. Apps that show "streak broken" imagery accelerate abandonment.
02
Onboarding friction is critical 78% of surveyed users abandoned a fitness app due to lengthy onboarding. The sweet spot for setup time is under 90 seconds.
03
Progress visibility drives retention Users who could see weekly improvement graphs (even marginal ones) were 3× more likely to return the following week.
04
Scheduling conflict is the #1 drop-off reason Life gets in the way. Users want intelligent rescheduling, not punishment for missing a day.
05
Social pressure is a double-edged sword 41% love accountability partners; 38% find social features anxiety-inducing. Opt-in social is essential.

Competitive Analysis

Competitor A

Nike Training Club

Excellent video library. Weak personalisation. No adaptive scheduling. Overwhelming content volume for beginners.

Competitor B

MyFitnessPal

Strong nutrition tracking. Fitness planning is bolted-on. Cluttered UI. Paywall frustration common in reviews.

Opportunity

FitFlow Gap

Adaptive scheduling + forgiving streak design + minimal onboarding = the underserved middle ground.

Section 05

Design Thinking Process

The five-stage Stanford d.school framework applied to FitFlow. Each stage informed the artifacts in this document.

Stage 01 — Empathise
Understand the Human

We conducted 12 user interviews and 84 surveys to map pain points, motivations, and existing behaviours. We shadowed 3 participants through their actual fitness app usage to observe drop-off moments in context rather than relying on self-reported behaviour.

12 Interviews 84 Surveys 3 Contextual observations Empathy maps Affinity diagram
Stage 02 — Define
Frame the Problem

From synthesis we arrived at a core problem statement: "Fitness app users lose motivation not because they don't want to exercise, but because apps make missing a session feel like failure rather than a normal part of life." Three personas were created to keep the design team grounded in real user needs.

HMW statements 3 Personas Point-of-view statements Jobs-to-be-done
Stage 03 — Ideate
Generate Solutions

A 90-minute collaborative ideation session produced 140+ ideas via Crazy 8s sketching. Key breakthrough ideas: adaptive streak logic that preserves momentum after missed sessions, and a sub-90-second onboarding. Ideas were voted on and clustered into feature priorities for v1.

Crazy 8s sketches Feature priority matrix Dot voting RICE scoring
Stage 04 — Prototype
Build to Learn

Low-fidelity wireframes (this document) provide the structural foundation. Next step is mid-fidelity prototypes in Figma with interactive transitions to enable usability testing. The wireframes prioritise layout decisions and information hierarchy without the distraction of colour or final copy.

User flows (3) Wireframes (6 screens) Figma mid-fi (next) InVision prototype (next)
Stage 05 — Test
Learn from Users

Planned: 5-person moderated usability tests using the mid-fidelity Figma prototype. Primary tasks to test: complete onboarding, start a workout, and recover from a missed session. Success metrics: task completion rate, time-on-task, and a post-session SUS score. Insights feed back into iteration cycles.

5-person usability test SUS score target ≥80 Task completion ≥85% Heuristic evaluation

Design Principles Established

Principle 01

Forgiveness over Punishment

Every missed session is a reschedule opportunity, not a failure state. Streak logic rewards consistency, not perfection.

Principle 02

Speed to Value

The user must reach their first completed workout in under 3 minutes from install. Onboarding earns the right to ask more questions later.

Principle 03

Visible Progress

Every screen should remind the user how far they've come. Progress charts, streaks and milestones are first-class UI elements.

Principle 04

Calm Clarity

No anxiety-inducing gamification. No dark patterns. The tone is a supportive coach, not a surveillance system. Clean, focused UI.
